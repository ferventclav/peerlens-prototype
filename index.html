How a session works

From an invite link to your own report

  1. Join with a link

    Open the invite, see what gets recorded, say yes, and pick the name others will see.

    S1CP1

  2. Read the same case

    Everyone reads the same case and exhibits, so you are all working on one problem.

    CP2

  3. Talk on your own mic

    Each person's voice is recorded separately, so every line has one clear speaker.

    CP3

  4. Get your own report

    First a transcript that shows who said what, then feedback that quotes your own lines with their times.

    CP4CP5CE

Our promises

Three things PeerLens always does

Proof

Every piece of feedback quotes something you said.

Each point shows your exact words and when you said them. If a line can't be traced to one person, it is left out.

Talk time

You see how long you spoke. There is no target.

It is there so you know, not to push you to talk more. Nobody gets hints during the discussion.

Privacy

Your report stays yours.

Recruiters never see it, and PeerLens does not score or rank anyone. It is practice feedback, just for you.

Kirana Konnect: expand to tier-2 cities or go deeper in metros?

Kirana Konnect delivers groceries in under 20 minutes from 140 dark stores across six metro cities. For the last six months it has also run one pilot store in Indore.

Metro order growth has slowed, and the board has money for one big push next year. It can open dark stores in tier-2 cities, or stay in the metros and add stores and categories there.

Your group's task: agree on one recommendation, and say what would change your mind.

Your goal this time: Back every challenge with one number from the case.

Statutory S3 Sample terms

Terms of use

The short version

  1. PeerLens is for practice. Your report is feedback for you. It is not used for hiring, and it is never shared with recruiters.
  2. Join only rooms you were invited to, from your own device, with headphones.
  3. Respect the people in your room. Do not record, copy or share anyone else's voice or words outside PeerLens.
  4. Feedback is drafted by AI and can be wrong. Every point quotes a turn, so you can check it against the transcript.
  5. We may remove someone from a room for abuse or harassment.
  6. You can leave a room at any time and ask us to delete your data, as the privacy notice explains.

Sample terms written for the prototype. A live service would publish full terms reviewed by a lawyer.
